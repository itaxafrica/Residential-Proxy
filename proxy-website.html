🛡️ What is a Residential Proxy?

A residential proxy is a real IP address assigned by an ISP to a real home. Websites see a legit household IP — not a server — making it impossible to detect or block you.

⚡ Why Residential?

Unlike cheap datacenter proxies, residential IPs come from real devices on real networks. They bypass CAPTCHAs, geo-blocks, and bot detection that would instantly flag anything else.
